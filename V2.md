# V2: Deferred Features

## Trading (deferred from v1)

Player-to-player pet trading is deliberately out of v1 scope. It is a
significant scam surface (middleman scams, social pressure on kids) and a
large dev cost (secure trade UI, atomic server-side swap, trade history,
anti-scam confirmations, support tooling).

When it lands in v2, minimum requirements:

- Atomic server-authoritative swap: both pets leave escrow together or
  not at all
- Confirmation screen both players must hold on
- Trade history log (for support and dispute resolution)
- New-player trade lock (account age / playtime minimum)
- Value-approximation display so kids can see when a trade is wildly
  lopsided

## Other v2 candidates

- Two-player board modes (Combo Pool's hotseat idea adapted to board spectate duels)
- Challenge / puzzle boards (fixed board states, fewest-launch solutions)
- Pet showcase trading floor in the lobby
- Guilds / team leaderboards
