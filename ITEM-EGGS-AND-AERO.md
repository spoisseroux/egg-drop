## What a player does

At the hatch station, choose between **Companion Egg** and **Hue Egg** in one grid. A player buys a Hue Egg for earned Stars, cracks it, and sees a glass jar containing a vivid pink berry. The result reads **Rose fruit · 3 in inventory**, including this jar. **Store** closes the receipt. **Use now** opens a confirmation with an owned pet, Main or Secondary, and the exact change. Close or disconnect at any stage after purchase and the jar is already safe. Applying a jar to Ovelle while Zivvi is equipped leaves Zivvi equipped.

Later, open **Pet Journal**, choose the clothing icon on Ovelle's row, then **Hue**. Switch between **Main** and **Secondary**. One generous portrait shows the saved pet. Four five-notch bars show the selected channel, above an inventory tray of counted jars. Tapping a jar asks **Use Rose fruit on Ovelle's Secondary? Rose 2/5 → 3/5 · 1 of 3 jars**. A small result swatch beside that text is enough. No dual portraits. Confirm commits exactly one use. Cancel leaves the camera, counts and pet alone. New cumulative color appears across the world, visitor view and portrait only after save confirmation.

## How jars paint a pet

Each pet has four integer counts, Rose/Tide/Citrus/Iris, **on each channel**. Each count runs from 0 to 5. Main paints the principal body surfaces. Secondary paints authored accents and the visible charm. Eyes, mouth, glints, teeth, cheek identity and rare sculpt markings have their own material roles. A sixth Rose on Main is rejected without spend even if other bars have room. Five Tide may still be applied to that Main. Each channel therefore holds up to 20 doses, with no eviction, dilution of stored counts or last-color replacement.

Use one deterministic resolver for cards, portraits, hatch, world, focus and visitors. A proposed exact mix has these parts.

1. Use the existing four vivid pigment anchors: Rose `#FF327A`, Tide `#008DFF`, Citrus `#FFCC00`, Iris `#9350FF`. Keep the six existing pair anchors (Rose+Tide violet `#AC4BFA`, Rose+Citrus coral `#FF733A`, Rose+Iris fuchsia `#E333BE`, Tide+Citrus green `#00BE77`, Tide+Iris blue-violet `#5859F1`, Citrus+Iris guava `#EE6997`). For art review start the four equal triples at Rose/Tide/Citrus `#F45688`, Rose/Tide/Iris `#A64FFF`, Rose/Citrus/Iris `#F059A5`, Tide/Citrus/Iris `#35A9CD`, and all-four at vivid orchid `#C963D9`. These are authored game colors, not a physical pigment simulation.
2. For notch levels 1 through 5, identify the hues reaching that level. Its target is the anchor for exactly that set, weighted by the number of hues in that set. Take the weighted mean in linear-light RGB. Example: Rose 3/Tide 1 = half Rose+Tide anchor, half pure Rose (two contributions from the first level and one each from the next two). Equal bars resolve exactly to their shared anchor. Permuting item order cannot change the resulting color.
3. Blend that target against the channel's authored original with the existing linear-light tint function. Strength at the **largest** count 0–5 is **0, .44, .65, .84, .93, 1**. A single fully dosed hue is its pure swatch; additional colors change its mixture. Rose 5/Tide 1 contains four-sixths pure Rose and two-sixths violet. Rose 5/Tide 5 is the full violet anchor. Hovering a jar never changes a public rig. Test all 1,296 count combinations per channel plus independent-channel/identity checks. Tune the proposed triple and all-four anchors against both rigs and report the resulting chip sheet.

**Reading and clearing a coat.** Use one row per hue, with a named fruit, five clearly separated equal-width ticks and a numeric count such as **Rose 3/5**. Indicate empty notches by outline, filled notches by color and fill, and the prospective notch by a small arrow on confirmation. This translates the user's purple segmented reference into counts that read at phone size. One **Reset Hue** clears every hue on **one selected pet and one selected channel**, restoring that channel's authored original. It consumes one Reset item. Clearing both channels needs two confirmations and two items. Show the lost bars and item count before confirmation. An already-original channel consumes nothing. It consumes exactly one Reset and preserves every other stack, charm visibility, equipment, ownership and Stars. No partial decrement, free Reset or automatic item use.

## Pet motion, night and PocketDeck controls

**Walking and facing.** The public path faces the endpoint chord even when the Bézier path initially moves another way. A half-turn currently takes about 1.68 seconds. A moving foot oscillates in local space and travels with the body. Give the pet a planted support foot in world space and let the other foot clear the ground. Drive phase by horizontal distance, cache the stance anchor across frames, release on toe-off and plant the next foot at a bounded reachable point. Yaw toward the actual horizontal travel tangent once moving. At near-zero speed retain last heading. On a reversal brake, pivot about the supporting foot, then accelerate. Target a 90° redirect in .15–.24 seconds and a 180° pivot in .28–.40 seconds after braking. Keep the head slightly ahead of the pelvis. Put compression at contact and shift the hips over the supporting foot before toe-off. Zivvi rocks between short planted feet. Compare a short planted waddle for Ovelle with a brief suspended beat driven by an obvious propulsive cuff/foot stroke, catch and settle. Use the waddle if the suspended version still reads as sideways drift. A floating personality buys no exemption from clear propulsion, quick facing and weight transfer.

Sample the public root, collision/path position and continuous heading **every rendered frame**. Trial **12 and 15 poses/second** for expressive face, head, cuffs and swing-foot poses against a smooth-pose control. Start at 15 Hz. Transform cached local poses with the fresh root every frame and keep stance-foot compensation continuous. No quantization of replication, camera, touch bounds, focus transitions or hit testing. Keep greetings, toy contacts and the return from focus synchronized to server action time. Rest and reduced motion suppress bounce/exaggeration while keeping heading and planted stance legible. Choose cadence after comparing fixed-camera captures side by side.

**Getting color through the night.** The v250 close-up has bright eye glints, while Zivvi's broad lower body reads gray and its feet nearly disappear. The current head uses an occluded Highlight at .70 transparency. A darkened Neon torso receives .43–.45 of source color. A stronger head-only overlay cannot solve weak torso/secondary separation. Existing valid MeshParts and native fallback geometry must each be tested.
