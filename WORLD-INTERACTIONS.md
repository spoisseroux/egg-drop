# Physical destinations in Sprout Hollow

Design v1 · 7 November 2026 · branch `feat/egg-drop-arcade-polish`. Source audit against `129f7df` and the concurrent UI working tree. Proposed build and test criteria follow. This review makes no claim of a completed Studio playtest.

**Yes. Give the existing hatch tree a browsable egg display, keep the two existing workstations, and keep hatch, pets and style reachable from the Pocket Menu.** A new player who sees eggs under the tree should be able to walk up and find out what one costs. ## What Roblox creators actually do

- **Adopt Me!** Uplift directed players to a gumball machine in the Nursery to acquire a Garden Egg. It later replaced the featured egg in the same location and explained that it wanted a familiar, calm Nursery for newcomers. The game's separate Pet Shop featured a vendor with hamster food. These examples support a recognizable place for a recognizable action. Our hatch tree already fills that role, so we can use it without building another interior. [Garden Egg release](https://www.playadopt.me/news/garden-egg-release-notes) · [Aztec Egg release](https://www.playadopt.me/news/aztec-egg-release-notes) · [Pet Shop update](https://www.playadopt.me/news/its-hamster-time)
- **Pet Simulator 99.** BIG Games placed the Supercomputer in the Tech World Shop to access other machines. Its later Supercomputer Radio let players access those machines through inventory after Rebirth 9. The same publisher also tied an egg to exploring Tokyo's alleys. Egg Drop can borrow the choice between a place and a shortcut. With a short replayable board loop, we should offer the shortcut as soon as egg browsing unlocks. [Tech World](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-6) · [Kawaii World](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-26)
- **Platform conventions.** Roblox explicitly calls a nearby E prompt a familiar interaction and supports native keyboard, controller and touch presentation, in-range gating and camera-dependent prompt exclusivity. Its onboarding guide emphasizes the first few minutes. Its discovery guidance names outcomes such as first-play bounce and repeat play. Neither these guides nor the two examples establish how common physical shops are across Roblox or predict that a shop will improve Egg Drop retention. ## Build order and player actions

1. **Connect the hatch tree.** Near the front stone rim, place a small, non-colliding, non-query sign or display part bearing the **Eggs** label. Attach a single standard prompt directly to a BasePart under the lounge, with `PocketClubStation = "Eggs"`, ObjectText **Hatch tree** and ActionText **Browse eggs**. An Attachment parent would bypass the current priority helper. Start at the nest's front edge, approximately (0, 5.5, −3.5) [PLACEHOLDER], so the label sits near eye level on the approach. Keep the center and camera sightlines clear. Try a 5-stud radius [PLACEHOLDER], shorter than the two tables' existing 7 studs. This reduces overlap while allowing a comfortable ground-level approach. Verify the actual three-dimensional boundary and adjust in Studio. Default native prompt, E/ButtonX, zero hold, clickable, one shared-key prompt, matching existing station line-of-sight behavior. Placing a small base underfoot must never block movement or camera collision.
2. **Open the shared egg route from free roam.** Reuse the existing egg-view open/navigation path in Room; no same-mode round trip or new remote is needed. Both Menu and station require loaded hatch state and at least one owned companion in the authoritative inventory mask. Use the existing `Pets.ownedCount(rank, trials, inventoryMask)` with the loaded mask. The legacy `OwnedPets` count represents the contiguous base-pet prefix and can be zero for a valid rare-only collection. Apply this eligibility rule to browsing and preserve existing server rules for welcome progression, room admission, pending hatches and transactions. Approach does nothing. Activating the prompt opens the same `EggHatchView` instance. Choosing a recipe previews it, and only its explicit Hatch action can request a purchase. Keep the current Earn route visible when short. X during a reveal preserves its ID. Meet and Play again use the existing acknowledgment flow. A stale snapshot or blocked route must not reopen a closed view, award anything or create a second listener. If the profile is loading, keep browsing unavailable until it is ready.
3. **Make the destinations readable in one glance.** From the entrance, the grove should read as an egg nest ahead, a book to the right and a pocket device under canvas to the left. Ground the signs on the objects. The existing Journal arrow can identify the fork. Only adjust it if actual entry views show that the nest or book is hard to find. Keep signs legible across the spawn-slot fan and under day/night lighting; distinguish the objects by shape as well as color. The egg should read as an egg at normal zoom. Avoid ceilings, doorways, camera pans and full-screen wayfinding. The field run remains accessible immediately from Deck.
4. **Verify the two existing routes alongside the new one.** A journal visitor can inspect a pet, deliberately equip an owned one or inspect Family goals. Opening goals leaves selected family and current run alone. A workbench visitor can preview and cancel at the bench or explicitly equip through the existing appearance request. Opening either station only navigates. If time is short, cut optional art upgrades first and retain interaction, clearance and restore checks.

The world build follows the verified UI slice. The boundary is one added browse interaction and local legibility fixes. The archived nursery, care timers, new purchases, currencies, daily limits and luck modifiers remain outside the build.

## Interaction ownership and restoration

- One shared client predicate should gate **both** prompt visibility and final dispatch. It requires local player, current room, live character, screen free, no switch/transition, no greeting, no game modal (including welcome/recap), and no Roblox menu/input capture. Recompute on mode, busy/focus/overlay/CoreGui ownership changes and newly added descendants. Check again at activation so a stale native event cannot open behind another owner. The existing station event guard and its enabled-state listeners cover different subsets; reconcile them in the approved UI integration instead of introducing competing handlers.
- In open roam, an enabled in-range station owns E/ButtonX. Keep a deliberate click/tap on the pet available via its existing ClickDetector. Opening a greeting hides station prompts; opening a station hides the pet hint and ClickDetector. When the player leaves station reach, the ordinary pet prompt returns. Retain the current dynamically maintained station cache; avoid a whole-lounge walk each frame. Avoid shared activation zones where a small camera turn changes which station answers.
- Browsing does not anchor, teleport or stage the avatar. Close returns to the current character and ordinary roam controls, with a valid controller selection or normal world input. Preserve position/orientation, camera subject/type and FOV, subject to ordinary player movement. Meet hands camera ownership to the greeting. Focus stays on the pet until Back restores the previous shot or a cancellation or teardown releases the camera. Earn/Play again intentionally returns to the board. Style Cancel restores the saved appearance. A mode switch, respawn, camera replacement or removed station invalidates pending callbacks against the prior owner. Respect CoreGui while it is open and reevaluate eligibility when it closes.
- Keep transaction checks in the existing server handlers. Browsing needs no authorization to mutate inventory because it changes no inventory. Validate costs, revision, eligibility, pending ID and save state on the same purchase/acknowledgment paths as today. Show ready/loading/short/error states from those paths. A session can safely use either route on either side of a retry. A second player has their own local prompts, currency, selection and movement.

## Convention review · v1.1, 7 November 2026

**Two overnight acceptance gates**

1. **Leave the screen with the same input used to open it.** Roblox documents direct touch on native prompts and directional navigation, select and Back on controllers. In the audited client the station handler and the ButtonB handler check the game's overlays differently, and the ButtonB handler has no explicit CoreGui ownership check. Verify whether a delivered Back press reaches the underlying game screen. Fold the CoreGui guard into the planned ownership fix. From the tree, open the shop by touch or ButtonX. Close using the visible corner X or ButtonB. Repeat with the Roblox menu over the shop: one Back should dismiss only the native menu, with the underlying shop and selected recipe intact. Recheck on a phone-sized screen with a controller connected. [Native prompts](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/proximity-prompts) · [Controller navigation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/console-guidelines) · [GuiService menu state](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/GuiService)
2. **Restore the purchase before offering another.** Roblox documents delayed final saves, stale sessions and communicating load failures. Egg Drop already has session fencing and persistent reveal IDs. Exercise the new entry point with a simulated delayed reconnect after one committed hatch. On acquisition, show the saved pet and balance. Opening from the tree or Menu must present the same pending result until acknowledged. A returning owner without a pending result can go directly to Deck. Record the fixture separately from a live rejoin. [Player data guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud-services/data-stores/player-data-purchasing) · [Existing recovery evidence](scripts/SessionRecovery-results.json)

**Design hypotheses to observe**

- **Discovery and accessibility.** Roblox gives in-world signs and contextual instruction as examples. Test whether the egg silhouette and short sign lead a newcomer from Pip to the shop without a tour. With sound muted, reduced motion on and enlarged text, check that the recipe name, price, shortfall and close control remain distinguishable. Defer an extra directional hint until observers see newcomers looking for eggs and repeatedly walking past them. [Onboarding techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques) · [Accessibility guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/accessibility)
- **A reason to share the grove.** Roblox recommends designing spaces where players can find each other. Sprout Hollow already displays nearby players' equipped companions. Keep the three destinations clustered so a friend can see the companion someone chose while each uses their own screen. All three activities should also work in an empty server. Defer additional social features until a two-player observation shows what people actually want to do together. [Roblox design guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) · [Existing companion display](src/client/RoomCompanions.lua)
