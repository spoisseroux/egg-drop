# V2 Candidate: Pinball Pet Collector (working title)

## Concept

A Pokemon Pinball (Ruby/Sapphire) style pinball game reusing the egg-drop
creature roster. Same pets, same rarity tiers, new game shell: launch a
ball into a themed table; hitting bumpers, loops, and egg targets charges
hatches; capture mechanics replace "catch 'em all" with a pet-collection
metagame kids already understand.

## Why this shape

- Second game shares the pet IP and art, cutting the biggest cost of a
  second title
- Pinball + cute collection is a proven GBA-era formula (Pokemon Pinball
  R/S sold well)
- Physics is already being built for the merge board; pinball reuses it

## Design references to iterate from (not Pokemon)

For creature design direction with big rosters of cute weird pets that
are NOT a huge IP:

1. **Slime Rancher** - plort/gordo slimes and chicken variants; simple
   round silhouettes, color+accessory differentiation at scale
2. **Temtem** - creature designs explicitly made to fill the
   "Pokemon-like but original" niche; strong archetype starters
3. **Monster Rancher / Monster Sanctuary** - readable cute archetypes
4. **Gexperia's catalog / Dragibar** - proven mid-tier Roblox pet shops;
   3D chibi style at production speed
5. **Palworld (early common-tier designs only)** - strong round-body
   cute-archetype design language, also Unreal source for target mix
6. **Pikmin** - silhouette-first, color-coded, instantly readable; best
   case study for "tell rarity without reading text"

## Status

Idea / not committed for v1. Do not build before egg-drop v1 launches
and retention data is in.
