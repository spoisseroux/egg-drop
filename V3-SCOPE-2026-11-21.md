### 2.3 "Next at N" on the stats panel

- **Detail strip** (exists, 54 px on phone, 116 px on desktop): under the "what trains it" line, one more line with a small picture: **"Next at 15: Rose Pom Beanie"**. A gated item adds a shop tag, a free reward adds "free". On desktop the strip has room for the next two.
- **It only shows where a row exists.** With medals at 25, 50, 75 and 99, every stat always has a next line until 99. At 99 it reads "Top level".
- **Stat tile:** a small lemon dot when a reward was earned since the panel was last opened.
- **Stat guide:** tapping the line opens the full list for that stat, 1 to 99: earned rows ticked, the next row lemon, later rows showing their level. Gated rows have a "Go to shop" key. Competition jobs are listed at the bottom ("Jump: clears hurdles in Garden Dash").

- Rewards are derived from levels, like name pieces. Nothing is written to the save for a free reward, so there is nothing to claim and nothing to lose.

## 4. Garden Dash

### 4.1 The game in one paragraph

Your pet runs the garden path by itself. You have two buttons: **Jump** (press as your pet reaches a hurdle) and **Dash** (hold to sprint while the lightning bar lasts). Jump well and the bar refills. Halfway, a signpost offers the **stepping stones**: a shorter way across the stream that needs three jumps in a row. First pet under the arch wins. Everyone finishes, everyone gets a ribbon, and it takes under a minute.

It is a rail race on purpose. The pet's place is one number (distance along the course), which the server owns. That makes it fair, cheap to check, the same on a phone and a gamepad, and readable by a six-year-old: there is no steering to get wrong.

### 4.2 The course

- **Route.** Start near the arrival end of the dirt path, over the marble bridge, finish at the edge of the hub plaza. `Routes.ArrivalToHub` covers about 130 studs of it; the course continues along the path past the hub to reach **200 to 240 studs [P]**. The exact line is measured from the walk map's path tiles in slice S5 and saved as a new `DashCourse` data module (points, heights from `groundY`, hurdle and fork positions).
- **It stays off the grove tiles** (around (-22, -32) to (-21, -48)) until the reroute is promoted.
- **Lanes are visual.** Eight pets are drawn side by side across the 4-stud path. Distance is counted on the centre line, so no lane is shorter on a bend.
- **Six hurdles [P]:** low hoops about 0.8 studs high, pet-sized. They rise out of the ground at the countdown and sink after the race. Players walk through them, so the path is never blocked.
- **The shortcut.** The main way crosses the marble bridge. The stepping stones cross the stream beside it: about 14 studs shorter (worth about 2.5 s) with three jumps. A missed stone is a splash and a 1.5 s climb out, so one miss makes it slower than the bridge. Every pet may take it at any level.
- **Art (Fable):** a start arch and a finish arch (one mesh, two banners), one hoop hurdle, three stepping stones, a fork signpost, eight lane flags, a small cup. All low-poly, built by script in the park equipment style. Sound: a countdown, a jump, a perfect chime, a splash, a dash whoosh, a finish fanfare. Composed in-house.

### 4.4 Stats into numbers [P]

Each stat has one job and gives **25% from level 1 to level 99, in a straight line**: `value = base x (1 + 0.25 x (level - 1) / 98)`.

| Stat | Job | Level 1 | Level 50 | Level 99 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Speed** | Running speed, studs a second | 6.0 | 6.75 | 7.5 |
| **Energy** | Dash bar, seconds of sprint (a dash is +30% speed for everyone) | 3.0 | 3.4 | 3.75 |
| **Jump** | Timing window for a Perfect, plus or minus seconds | 0.12 | 0.135 | 0.15 |

| Jump result | Window at level 1 | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect | within 0.12 s | No slowdown, +0.5 s of dash bar, a chime |
| Clear | within 0.30 s (also 25% wider at 99) | A small stumble: 10% slower for 0.4 s |
| Trip | outside, or no press | A tumble: 1.2 s, then back up to speed |

- A clean run on a 220-stud course is about 34 s at level 1 and about 28 s at 99. A typical run is about 40 s.
- **With the same play, an all-99 pet finishes about 20% sooner than an all-1 pet.** Bands (4.5) keep those two from meeting when stats count. Inside a band the widest gap is about 8% [P], which is two trips. So in a fair heat, hands beat stats.
- Just for Fun runs every pet at the level 50 numbers.
- Boost Treats, favourite stats, wearables, capes and passes change nothing in a race. Racing gives **no stat XP**. You train to race.

### 4.4b A bit of luck [P]

Spencer, 2026-11-21: "add a bit of randomness to who wins". Three small things, all drawn on the course so a child can point at why it happened. There are no hidden rolls: nothing random ever touches a jump window, a speed or a result behind the scenes.

| Luck | What happens | What it is worth |
|---|---|---|
| **Course of the day** | Each heat uses one of four authored hurdle layouts: Even, Pairs (two hoops close together, twice), Early (four before the fork) and Late (four after it). Same six hoops, same total length, the same for every runner. | Nothing by itself. It changes which heat suits which player, so the same person does not win every time. |
| **Lucky clover** | A four-leaf clover grows just past one hurdle in two of the lanes (one lane in a 2 or 3 runner heat). It is in place at the countdown, so everyone sees who has one. Your pet picks it up only if it Cleared or Perfected that hurdle; a Trip tumbles past it. | +1.0 s of dash bar: about 2 studs, 0.3 s. |
| **Zoomies** | Once a heat, somewhere between 40% and 70% of the course, the pet in last place gets the zoomies if it is at least 6 studs behind the pet in front: a rainbow puff, ears back, +20% speed for 2 s. | About 2.7 studs, 0.4 s. Less than the gap it needs, so zoomies alone never passes anyone. |

Rules:

- **Lanes are dealt, not chosen.** The pad you stand on does not set your lane. The server deals lanes at the countdown, so nobody can stand where the clover will be.
- **The luck budget.** The most luck one runner can get in a heat is about 0.7 s (clover plus zoomies), under 2% of a 40 s run and well under one Trip (1.2 s). A run that is one Trip cleaner always wins. Luck only decides heats that were close anyway.
- **Target:** in a heat of four evenly banded runners, the one who played best (fewest Trips, then most Perfects) wins **70 to 80% of the time** [P]. Slice S5 checks this by running 10,000 seeded heats through `DashSim` with the pals' skill rolls, and the clover and zoomies sizes are tuned until it holds. If it reads above 85%, add a third clover lane before raising any size.
- **Seeded by the server, once per heat.** The seed is drawn when the countdown starts and joining has closed, so leaving and rejoining cannot reroll it. It picks the layout, the lane deal, the clover lanes and hurdle, and the zoomies moment. Clients are sent the layout and clovers (they are visible anyway) but never the seed or the zoomies moment. The seed is saved with the heat result, so any heat can be replayed from seed plus inputs for a check.
- **It never pays.** No Stars, no XP, no ribbon of its own. Ribbons follow the finish order as before. Pals get the same luck as players.
- **Just for Fun has more:** clovers in half the lanes, and zoomies lasts 3 s and can fire twice. **Stats On** uses the table above. **Time Trial has none** and always runs the Even layout, so par times, cups and ghosts stay comparable.
- **Racing is free.** No entry cost in Stars or anything else, in any mode.
- Reduced Motion: zoomies keeps the speed and the ears, drops the rainbow puff.

Art and sound (Fable, added to the 4.2 list): one clover prop, one rainbow puff sprite, a clover chime and a zoomies whirr. Build: layouts and the seed live in `DashCourse` and `DashSim` (S5); the server deals and records them (S6); the client draws them (S7).

### 4.5 Lobby and matchmaking (12 players a server)

- **The start gate** is a place in the garden with eight pads and a sign. Step on a pad with your pet out, or use the sign's key.
- **One heat at a time per server.** The first player to step up picks the mode. A 20 s gather countdown [P] starts and resets to at least 8 s when someone joins. Most heats will be 2 to 4 players, and that is fine.
- **Stats On:** the heat takes the first player's band, and only pets in that band can join it. Others see "This heat is for Sprout racers. The next Just for Fun heat is open to everyone." and can queue for it.
- **Bands** [P]: by **Dash level = Speed + Energy + Jump** (3 to 297): 3 to 29, 30 to 74, 75 to 149, 150 to 224, 225 and up, named with the tier words players know (Seedling to Starbloom). Spencer agreed "total-level bands"; I recommend the three race stats instead, because a pet with Heart 99 and Speed 5 should not be pushed up among fast pets (question 4).
- **Garden pals** fill empty lanes up to four runners. They are Bears and Bunnies in fixed recolours with pet names from the name pieces and a small leaf tag, never dressed as players. They run the same simulation with a skill roll (Perfect on 40 to 80% of jumps [P]) and the band's middle stats. They can win.
- **Time Trial:** any time, no waiting, even during a heat. Your pet runs alone at Just for Fun stats, only you see it, and your best run is a ghost. Par times pay cups: Bronze 48 s, Silver 43 s, Gold 39 s [P].
- No cross-server matchmaking in v3. A server is the lobby.

Place does not change the Stars, so there is nothing to gain from win-trading or farming pals. A ranked "Dash Champion" title needs the global board store and comes with it in v3.1.

### 4.7 Fairness and cheating

- **The server runs the race.** It holds every runner's distance, speed and dash bar and steps them on its own clock. A shared pure module (`DashSim`) runs on both sides, the pattern the Pocket Deck already uses: the client predicts its own pet so buttons feel instant, and the server's result is the result.
- **Clients send only button events** (jump, dash down, dash up, take stones) with a time. The server judges a jump against where it says the pet is. It allows for lag up to 150 ms [P] and no further, drops inputs faster than 10 a second, and ignores anything from a player not seated in the heat.
- **Stats are read from the save on the server at the gate.** The public attribute is never an input.
- Avatar speed and teleport exploits do nothing: the avatar is not racing, and pet actors are server-owned as in the park.
- **Away players:** a runner with no input still finishes (slowly). Stars need at least three inputs and a finish inside twice the Gold par.
- A heat ends 20 s after the winner. Pets still running are placed by distance. A player who leaves mid-heat is replaced by a pal at the same spot.
- Daily counts and ribbons are written in one transaction per heat with an operation id, like every other grant.

### 4.8 How it reads to a child

- Two buttons with pictures (a spring, a lightning bolt). No text is needed to play.
- 3, 2, 1, GO with the pets bouncing on their pads.
- A trip is funny, not a failure: a tumble, a shake, back up. Nobody is knocked out.
- The results card says "You finished 5th!" with a ribbon and your best time. There is no losing screen, no red and no rank going down.
- Only pet names and display names are shown. No race chat prompts, no taunt emotes.
- The first race for any player is a Just for Fun heat with pals, with the ring on the Jump button drawn larger.

## 9. Colour and material rework (Spencer, 2026-11-21)

Spencer: "greatly improving the materials and hue systems, it's a bit confusing and lame now". In v3 scope; it needs a Fable creative brief before any build. Its place in the release order is still to be decided (question 13).

### 9.1 What players get today (checked in code)

- **Colour (Hue):** 4 jars (Rose, Tide, Citrus, Iris), hatched at random from the 12-Star Customization Egg. Using a jar adds one dose of that colour to the pet's Main or Secondary colour (0 to 5 per colour). The result is a hidden blend: the mix anchors in `HueCatalog.resolveTarget`, weighted by dose. Jars are used up. "Original colour" is free.
- **Material:** 3 jars (Gloss, Matte, Jelly) from the same egg. They move a read-only Shine / Jelly meter (`MaterialPanel`). Metal, Pearl Edge and Speckle Satin exist in data but are not hatchable.
- Both live in Customize beside Charm, and the same egg also drops the charms.

### 9.2 Why it likely reads as confusing and lame (to confirm with Spencer)

1. **You can't choose a colour.** You get random jars, and you can't tell what colour a dose will give until you've used it.
2. **Jars are used up.** A colour you liked isn't something you keep; you have to rebuild it from doses.
3. **Main vs Secondary is not obvious.** The only place Secondary shows clearly is the charm.
4. **The range is small:** four pigments and their blends, with no patterns or markings, and no pastel, dark or glitter options.
5. **Material is a meter, not a look.** "Shine 2/5" doesn't sound like something you'd want, and only three jars exist.
6. **Payoff is low.** A whole-body tint change is subtle on a small pet in the garden.

### 9.3 Directions for Fable to brief (proposals, none approved)

- **Keep what you unlock.** A jar unlocks a swatch (or a family of shades) for good, and you then pick from your palette for free, the way outfits work. Doses go away.
- **Preview before you commit.** Every swatch and material previews on the pet before you use it, with a named result ("Lagoon", "Plum").
- **More to collect:** pastel, deep and special shades; markings and patterns (spots, socks, ear tips, belly) on the Secondary colour; materials that read at garden distance (sparkle, glow-trim, fuzzy, the parked Metal and Pearl).
- **One clear Look screen:** body colour, markings colour, material, then charm, with saved looks you can switch between.
- **Fair-play rules still hold:** odds shown, no duplicates where possible, the restricted-account fixed order, Stars only.

### 9.4 Constraints for the build

- Saves hold `hues` ranks, `petHues` doses and finish unlocks. Every look a player has today must still show after the change, and any move to a new model has to convert it at load without a failed load.
- Jars that players already hold, but haven't used, must stay worth something: they convert to unlocks, or they refund.
- Book pages, quests and the egg odds disclosure that mention jars all change together.

### 9.5 Next steps

1. Fable: a short creative brief with 2 or 3 directions shown on the real Bear and Bunny (captures, not mock-ups).
2. Spencer picks one.
3. Sol: a plan with a save migration, then the review-driven build, beta first.

## 10. A living garden, even alone (Spencer, 2026-11-22)

Spencer: "for v3 we need to make the 3d world feel a lot more alive even when no one is there". In v3 scope. It matters for launch: the listing showed 0 players online when this was written, so most visitors will be alone. Everything under 10.2 is a proposal until Fable's brief and Spencer's pick.

5. **Sound to match:** creature and resident sounds under the new quieter water levels.

### 10.3 Rules

Client-side and cosmetic where possible; a hard cap on live creatures; phone frame rate and Reduced Motion checked per piece; residents share the Dash pals' code so they are built once; original art only; no pretending there are more players than there are.
