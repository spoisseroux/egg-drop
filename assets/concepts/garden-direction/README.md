## Prompt set

**Silhouette sheet:** Original simple Y2K baby designer toys for a Roblox pet garden. Three large full silhouettes and two expressive poses each: a squat seedling quail with tiny beak/leaves, an aquatic pebble calf with fan ears/flippers/paddle tail, and a lantern-seed cub with folded petal ears/honey belly. Floating pearl dot accessory, avatar ruler, quarter-to-third avatar body scale, sparse labels and day/night palette strip. Avoid recognizable Chao anatomy, Sonic branding, copied faces, realistic fur and plush fabric.

**Customization sheet:** Preserve the first original Pip; identical base pose with separated Dot, Spike, Ring and Heart accessories. Show cloth, bag, trail, pearl and stripe candidates. Same pet at day and night, colored body readable without blown-out Neon, face protected, all cosmetic changes without stat advantage. Clean catalog layout with cyan/coral/lime/cobalt accents.

**Revised material sheet:** Use the first sheet only for original pet identities, the supplied Fire Talk image only for its chalk/sky/grass/acid-yellow/flame-orange/magenta/charcoal palette, and sampled second-video frames only for broad white/cobalt/magenta material lobes. Simplify to satin low-poly-friendly Y2K designer toys; reduce blush and white forehead gloss. Show golden Pip, cyan Puddle, coral Wick; identical Pip in satin/Pearl Edge/Spectrum/Ink Chrome; original open green garden with chalk/lilac enclosure, cyan pool, saffron lanterns, tiny avatar and legible night pet. Do not copy reference subjects, logos, brushwork, rabbit anatomy or Chao silhouette. Sparse graphic typography on chalk white.
