## Build a place pets can actually use

A new visitor should see a low creek crossing, two small pets playing on grass, and a bench player within one camera turn. Walk across the creek, send your pet's wobble ball through a low gate, watch another player's pet return it, then open the handheld. Give the central clearing long uninterrupted views of faces at avatar-knee height. The outer boardwalk and orchard can feel expansive without putting another menu between players and their pets.

Use the existing 160×128-stud floor, Y = 4 top, bridge near (17.6, −9.4), cascade near (17, −19), benches at (±11.8, 12.6), and central lawn/ball at (−2, −4)/(−2, −9). The outer boardwalk and orchard already have paths and lamps. Extend these landmarks and redistribute existing scenery before enlarging the floor. Verify the actual spawn orientation and station extents in Studio before positioning new scenery. Add **Ring** and **Pebble** as immediately selectable cosmetic shapes alongside the existing **Dot** in the same Journal Charm tab. Ring is a narrow oval hole with a rounded edge. Retain bounded unknown shape IDs opaquely in storage with a render-only Dot fallback, and restrict new requests to explicitly owned shapes. Phase out current Dot-only writers before enabling new selections; a legacy writer must never replace a saved Ring with Dot. Test owner/visitor, rejoin, latency and rapidly alternating shape/visibility on different pets.
