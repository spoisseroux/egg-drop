## Pet mechanics

1. **Tap and rub.** Preserve held focus and Back. Remove the separate Greet button, its default controller selection, and its GUI references. A deliberate tap on the projected pet produces a visible grin; a held moving stroke sustains petting and a quiet purr. Release, leaving the pet, interruption or teardown ends the purr. Use the existing pointer owner so a tap and a stroke do not double-trigger on release. Maintain E/ButtonX as an in-focus reaction for keyboard/controller and ButtonB for Back. Give the controller a reachable pet action without moving focus onto a removed widget. Make the face readable during reduced motion without requiring the bounce. Keep existing reward, equip, camera and focus state intact.
2. **Teach once per client session.** Present the rub hint on the first eligible focus encounter; retire it after the first accepted pet interaction or leaving that first encounter. Keep the nearby pet hint concise and retire it after first successful greeting. Swapping pets, backing out, resizing or changing input does not repeatedly restore either lesson. A save notice using the same well must remain visible whenever it owns presentation. A new joined session can teach again without a data-store change. A reconnect-persistent preference would be separate scope.
3. **Gait and size.** Have the lounge factory pass an explicit scale to the visual constructors; default to 1 for other consumers. Scale static part sizes, stored rest translations, animated pivots and vertical excursions uniformly about the floor origin. Roblox Model scaling changes descendant transforms, but these rigs overwrite transforms from authored rest data during rendering. A one-time model scale on its own cannot preserve their animation. Keep face marks and feet aligned throughout breathing, blinking, greeting and turning. Use the horizontal displacement of the visual after follow smoothing to advance stride distance; stop stride at rest and reset phase on teleport, snap or respawn. Pip and Wick can alternate feet, Puddle ripple/squash, Orbit bob with its ring. Keep motion subtle and reduce nonessential bobbing when reduced effects is on. Share gait between the local pet and the existing maximum six displayed nearby companions. Preserve the 10Hz ground sampling and existing draw budget; do not raycast every foot or allocate instances per step. [Follow](src/client/PetFollow.lua) · [Nearby companions](src/client/RoomCompanions.lua) · [Model scaling](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Model#ScaleTo)
4. **Rank crest.** Today's billboard is 46×26 pixels, offset (1.05,0.55,0) from the body, depth-tested and limited to 30 studs. Center its attachment above the top of the complete animated silhouette, including sprig, flame or halo. Use real projected top and bottom of the badge to check for vertical separation; a fixed stud offset alone can overlap when zoomed out or camera pitched down. Budget enough headroom in the greeting composition for the whole plate; keep the 3D subject and its pointer hit box separate from presentation adornments. Retain depth occlusion so an intervening wall hides the badge. Hide the local badge when a safe placement cannot fit or its owner is offscreen, then restore it when clear. Remote badges retain range and pet-count limits. Rank 0 has no crest; ranks I–III retain existing identity. [Prestige](src/client/PetPrestige.lua) · [Greeting framing](src/client/PetGreetingShot.lua) · [Billboard depth behavior](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/BillboardGui#AlwaysOnTop)
5. **Purr ownership.** Start with one quiet, auditioned voice for the active local pet; handle silent/failed asset, SFX mute and device pause without blocking the grin. If looping, start only on accepted contact and stop/fade on release, loss of pointer/window focus, Back, CoreGui, text entry, open sheet, mode change, character/camera replacement, pet replacement or destroy. No sound should restart from an old callback. Keep it inside the existing sound group. Include one-voice and zero-residual-voice assertions; audible quality needs a real audition. ## Station prompts and roam controls

First try the default prompt with one short verb phrase, reduced redundant object text and a modest `UIOffset` toward the station. Roblox exposes offset, style, reach and exclusivity; it does not expose a native prompt font-size or scale property. Compare the saved camera angle with the supplied touch screenshot. If it still covers the face or obscures the service object, implement custom **presentation only** for the three tagged stations. Use Roblox PromptShown/Hidden and current input type with recognizable key/button glyphs. Retain the real prompt's E/ButtonX, zero hold, nearest-winner exclusivity and final activation guard. For touch/mouse, one custom button calls the native prompt input begin/end path; never call the destination handler a second time. Anchor toward the object with one line whenever legible; target remains at least 44×44 logical pixels, grows/wraps at Largest, and fits CoreGui-safe content. Remove UI and retained connections on hide/removal, CoreGui ownership and surface teardown; the floating control cannot intercept pet clicks outside its own target. Compare old/new at the same camera distance and actual emulator resolution. [Roblox proximity prompts](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/proximity-prompts) · [ProximityPrompt API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ProximityPrompt) · [Current dispatch](src/client/LoungeStations.lua)

Replace the shared rectangular roam panel with two distinct 44px-or-larger circle targets, using the hamburger and Pocket Deck silhouette inside them. Give each a readable edge over sunlit grass and dark sky, accessible Menu and Open deck labels, a controller focus ring and sufficient separation. Retain existing open handlers and station/overlay/Back ownership. Recompute the optional first-use pet hint and save notice placement below the new dock. Avoid the Roblox topbar, jump and thumbstick; compare both phone orientations. The companion interaction area stays clear of the dock. Keep the original first-entry camera, free orbit and exact Deck-return shot until measured same-camera before/after images show a framing issue, then adjust only the affected first placement. ## Acceptance

- Record equivalent camera and avatar measurements before/after at tree, journal and workbench, plus a side-by-side local pet and a pet greeting. Desktop and actual Studio phone layouts must show both a recognizable service object and the avatar's face/upper body. Test idle room, touching a station, close, direct pet tap alongside a station, rotate, and keyboard/Gamepad1/touch. Record physical-device and human observations separately.
- All seven species test flat ground, bridge/bench edge, standstill → walk → turn → stop, low render rate and long snap. Check feet/skirt relative to ground and complete body/clothing/face after several updates, old hatch/reveal scale, remote pet scale, badge + animated silhouette, rank 0 and I–III, reduced effects and a real solid occluder. Ensure local pet remains tappable after shrinking; keep at least a 44px projected focus target where there is room, distinct from Back/controls. Compare greeting camera aspect, occlusion planning and saved camera restore.
- Verify tap grin, continuous rub/purr and stop on every cancellation. Verify the rub hint is shown once, save-status text can take its place, Gamepad1 E-equivalent/X and B still work, no hidden Greet focus, no accidental activation entering focus, and no regression to the separate existing four-sheet controller takeover. Repeat with another pet and after respawn. If a gesture lands outside a valid pet silhouette or the camera has no safe greeting shot, stay in current valid roam/Back flow.
- Retain existing greeting lifecycle/camera, station priority, navigation and transaction tests. Add exact regressions for world-only scale surviving pose updates, one badge above the full projected silhouette, rank occlusion, first-hint retirement across repeated visits, no duplicate reaction/voice and zero stale prompt controls/listeners. Record low-frame-rate gait smoothness and audio behavior as observed; do not infer physical phone comfort from emulation.
