# Sparse garden texture audition

A 24-second local listening draft following Astra's sparse audition brief. Nothing is uploaded, installed or connected to runtime audio.

[Stereo WAV](garden-texture-audition-24s.wav) · [Stereo MP3](garden-texture-audition-24s.mp3) · [Mono WAV](garden-texture-audition-mono-24s.wav) · [Sample-bank strip](sample-bank-strip.wav).

Stereo measures **−37.19 LUFS / −15.49 dBTP**; mono measures −40.21 LUFS / −15.95 dBTP. No final gain normalization was applied. Mono differs from stereo per-channel RMS by only −0.007 dB; the LUFS channel-count difference is not phase cancellation.

The character comes from recorded rubber squeaks and three plops. Original additions are a few hollow taps, a soft rattle, one quiet F2 and the distant A4–C5–D5 fragment. There are no drums, ostinato, flute lead, continuous sample loops or copied soundtrack material. Foreground samples occupy 9.3% of the timeline, with at most two overlapping. The small room uses a 14 ms predelay, 550 ms T60 and 14% send.
