# Architect review: Hidden Grove build plan (2026-11-28)

Plan: `GROVE-BUILD-PLAN.md`. Verdict: **GO WITH CHANGES.** Look and pipeline right; seven wiring points.

1. **Hook:** GroveWorld runs inside AeroWorld's lifecycle (shared room binding, turn tokens): build in `World:bind` after `buildKit`/`buildAdditions` (AeroWorld.lua:404-405), drop in `disable` (:572), connect its switch in `enable` like `sceneChanged` (:553-560). Parent to workspace folder `EggDropGrove` (like `EggDropAero`, AeroAdditions.lua:14), never inside EggDropLounge (`World:paint` recolours Beams to `Values.Falls`, :314-315; WindClient scans room folders).
2. **Backdrop hides:** own records table, not `self.hidden` (`dropKit` restores all of `self.hidden` at budget level 2 and scene flips, :521, :527-528, :557). Hide with `LocalTransparencyModifier` on parts and their Texture/Decal children (Transparency has three writers: paint :262-265, egg corner, WorldMotion pulse :142/:190). Re-apply in `queue`'s late pass (:341-345) for streaming re-creation. Add a skip in WorldMotion (:172-174) so hidden parts stop stepping. Also hide `W7_CastleFlag_1..9` (they float once peaks go). **Keep `W7_Underside__cliff_rock`** until a bank mesh seals the band between the cliff bottoms (y about -8, `W7_Rocks__cliff_rock`) and the moat.
3. **Sky Falls:** cut short, not extended (server beam to y -68, WorldV7Data `beams.falls`; AeroFalls strand/ribbon 28/22 studs from lip y 3.25, AeroFalls.lua:352-359). Disable `Water_SkyFalls` in grove records, hide `W7_Water__cloud`, pass moat height into `Additions.build` (:428).
4. **Switch:** an Alive feature `grove`, missing = off (like `windbed`, Alive.lua:41-48): ceiling, `alive_grove` key and `/alive grove on` come free (AliveConfig.lua:32). On = `Aero.on()` and `Alive.on("grove")`. Budget: AeroWorld's `self.level` (only rises; Studio always 0, AliveBudget.lua:40: specs/Studio need an `options.level` seam). Reduced Motion: AeroWorld's `self.reduced` (:152-156), not WorldMotion's (:117).
5. **Templates:** clients get meshes only as rbxmx templates mounted in ServerStorage and copied to ReplicatedStorage where the ceiling passes (AeroConfig.lua:52-67, default.project.json:74-79). Add `GroveKit.rbxmx`, unique `GV_` names, preload before hiding anything (like `buildKit` :479-505), CollisionFidelity Box.
6. **Meshes/textures/shadows:** 10k tris per mesh is safe; four 1024 textures ~22 MB worst case; the risk is overdraw and draw calls. Colour `TextureID` is enough (normal maps only via a template-authored SurfaceAppearance, AeroKit.lua:72-84). Far band: 8 opaque sector meshes ≤ 2k tris; no alpha impostors or alpha leaf cards (sorting against Beams/Clouds). `CastShadow` false on the whole ring (low sun at 7.1 and 16.9 would shade the lawn). Sun gate aimed with `GetSunDirection()` at ClockTime 10.5.
7. **Rays/night:** Beams with `FaceCamera`, 1 segment, `LightInfluence` 0; end above the lawn and away from the glass EggDome (AeroAdditions.lua:28-34) and falls sheets; ≤ 5 on phones. Fade by GardenHour phase (GardenHour.lua:144-162): full in the day hold, cyan in the night hold, 0 at dawn and golden hour, blended by `k`; hour off: `HollowClock.daylight()`. Don't write `HollowSunRays` (paintLight owns it, :218): add a multiplier through `self.look`. Lanterns Neon only, no new PointLights (DriftLanterns uses 4); lit when `DriftLanterns.lit` (daylight < .3, :249). The `high_falls` balloon (DriftLanterns.lua:167) flies near the east rim: spec the balloon tops against the crown undersides.

Specs: budgets plus clearances: moat `surfaceY` ≤ -25 (or sky layers clamp), every mid-band piece ≥ 45 studs past the rim, balloon tops under the canopy.
