Hatch a pal on your handheld, then raise it in the garden. Merge balls on your Pocket Deck and your first pal, the Bear, cracks out of its egg.

YOUR FIRST MINUTE
• Drop balls and merge two of a kind into a bigger one.
• A few merges crack the egg. Your Bear hatches with Stars to spend.
• Say hi in the garden, then hatch its first toy.

EVERY DAY
• Welcome bonus: extra Stars on your first three rounds.
• Raise your pal: name it, feed it snacks, train six stats, dress it up and dye it.
• Daily stamp, new quests and Stat Eggs every day. Stamp 7 brings home a Bunny.
• Play in the Pet Park with other players' pets.
• Restyle your deck with faceplates and ball themes.

FAIR PLAY
• Every egg shows its odds before you hatch it.
• Optional Robux Star packs and passes only add Stars. Nothing you buy changes how the board plays.
• No trading.
