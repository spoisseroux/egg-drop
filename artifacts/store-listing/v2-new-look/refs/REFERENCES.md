## Chao Garden mods (SA2 PC, GameBanana)

- [Enhanced Chao World for SA2](https://gamebanana.com/) ([full image](https://images.gamebanana.com/img/ss/mods/622400332adec.jpg)), "Extended Chao Garden (Neutral)", "Meridell Chao Garden" and "HeartGold Garden" were found through an image search for "sa2 chao garden mod hd screenshot".
- **Grass:** saturated emerald, close to #1FB82E to #3FD23A. It is flat and smooth, like a lawn carpet, with slightly lighter tufts. There is no muddy brown anywhere.
- **Water:** a clear turquoise channel, around #49B6E8 to #7FD6F2, with pale lilac-grey stone kerbs. You see the bed through it and get a white glint on the edge.
- **Sky:** a soft gradient from pale yellow-mint at the horizon (#F3F7C8) up to clean cyan (#5FD0F0). The horizon is a sea of pink-lilac cotton clouds (#F6C9E6).
- **Props:** pale violet and white marble arches, palm trees with lime fronds, round fruit in yellow and orange, and a big chunky fern in the foreground corner.
- **Light:** bright, shadowless midday with low contrast. Every colour sits at high value.
- **Why it works:** two or three clean saturated fields (grass, water, sky), pastel accents and toy-scale props. That is the "Chao Garden" feeling Spencer wants.

## Frutiger Aero devices (image search: glossy MP3 player concepts, keychain players)

This set is the faceplate brief.

- **Translucent candy plastic:** a cobalt rim (#1C5BD8) fading to sky (#6EC3FF) fading to near-white at the top. You can see depth into the shell.
- **One big glass highlight band:** a white highlight sweeps across the top third and fades to clear. A thin bright rim line follows the outline.
- **Clouds reflected in the plastic** on some designs: soft white cumulus inside the shell.
- **Pill and round buttons with glossy caps:** a white top spec and a darker bottom lip.
- **Silver-white bezel ring** round the screen, with a soft inner shadow.
- Variations seen: aqua and white, lime and white (keychain), smoke-clear with a blue tint.
