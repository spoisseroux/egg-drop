Pocket Pals is a cozy merge game with cute pets to raise. Merge balls on your Pocket Deck to earn Stars, then dress up, train and play with your pals.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Pocket Deck: drop balls and merge two of a kind into a bigger one. Chain merges, finish goals, win Stars. Easy, Medium or Hard.
• Two pals: your Bear, and a Bunny you can adopt.
• Hatch eggs for toys, dye jars, finishes, charms, names and stat snacks.
• Train six stats: Heart, Power, Energy, Jump, Speed and Smarts.
• Dress up with hats, shirts, glasses and more, and restyle your deck with faceplates and ball themes.
• Pet Park: swings, slide and see-saw with other players' pets.
• Quests, daily stamps and a collection book.

FAIR PLAY
• Every egg shows its odds before you hatch it.
• Optional Robux Star packs and passes only add Stars. Nothing you buy changes how the board plays.
• No trading.
