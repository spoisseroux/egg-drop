# Egg Drop trailer: shot list (45–55 s)

For Spencer to record and upload. This file only plans the shots, so nothing has been recorded yet. Use real gameplay only: OBS, or Roblox's own recorder (Esc menu → Record → Record Video on Windows). Don't use AI video, and don't use footage or art from outside the game.

## Roblox video rules (Creator Hub, read 13 November 2026)

- **Allowed:** varied camera angles, minor brightness or contrast tweaks, a logo or branding overlay, curated highlights of real mechanics, in-game UI text, and Roblox catalog music.
- **Not allowed:** gameplay or themes that aren't in the game, visuals beyond what players see, real-life footage or outside overlays, and **any spoken audio**: no voice-over, narration or voice chat, and **no music with lyrics**.
- Use text overlays sparingly and only to describe what's on screen. No ads or subjective claims ("best", "50 % off", "#1").
- Uploads are free but limited to **3 a month**, and rejected videos count toward that. Every video is reviewed. Approved videos show first on the detail page. They don't play on Xbox, PlayStation or VR, so the image thumbnails still matter.

## Setup

- **Resolution:** the Roblox player at 1920 × 1080, windowed full screen, 60 fps, graphics quality at maximum. Record 16:9.
- **Account:** record from an account that **doesn't own the passes**, so results show what most players earn. Your own account shows the 2× and Supporter rows, and the "Win pays" numbers come out higher.
- **Second account** (your Android phone or your friend from ONBOARDING.md) for the Pet Park duo shot. Two players' pets need to be in the park together.
- **Camera:** most shots use the game's own camera. For the garden and park tracking shots, use Roblox Freecam (Shift+P works for place owners and developers in Studio Play and in the live game). Move slowly, with no snap cuts on the camera.
- **Hide the Roblox top bar** for the world shots if you can. If you can't, crop it out in the edit, since that counts as minor post-processing.
- **Studio alternative:** Studio Play plus OBS works for every shot except the duo. Studio's test grants (`EggDropStoreTestGrant`) can fund the wardrobe shots without spending Robux.
- **Audio:** record the game's own sound effects. Music options, in order of preference:
  1. **Spencer's own garden track**, if it has no lyrics and is uploaded to Roblox under your account.
  2. The game's current **Pocket Deck placeholder loop** (`assets/audio/placeholder/egg_drop_deck_music.wav`, asset 114810234817689) under the deck shots, crossfading to the in-game garden ambience (brook, birds) for the world shots.
  3. A lyric-free track from the Roblox audio catalog.

  No voices of any kind.

## Avoid

- Pass rows or "×2" results from an account that owns the passes. They show earnings most players won't see.
- The Studio test grant toast, Studio's UI, dev-console text or Freecam's on-screen hints.
- Any feature that isn't in the published build (the Feature Egg while `Features=false`, the day/night cycle while `DayNight=false`, unreleased pets).
- Text over the bottom ~15 % of the frame, where player metadata can cover it.

## After recording

1. Trim to 30–60 s and export as H.264 MP4, 1920 × 1080, 60 fps.
2. Watch it once with the sound on: game sounds and lyric-free music only.
3. Creator Hub → Egg Drop → Places/Thumbnails → add video. The upload counts toward the 3-a-month quota.
4. Once approved, keep image thumbnails 1, 3, 4 and 5 active for personalization, since the video doesn't reach console players.
