# Sleepy Nest renders, review R1 (Fable, 2026-11-24)

Seen: all nine state renders and `render-beside-machine.png`. Colours are judged under the render's sun, so check each hex against the atlas before repainting.

## Verdict: GO WITH CHANGES

The pot works. Bowl, feet, band and collar read as the machine's sibling, and the size beside the machine is right (crook top just under the globe top, mass much lower). Three things stop the export: the crook and awning read as a palm tree, the tucked blanket reads as a bib, and the nest reads as dark thorny chocolate.

## Changes, most important first

1. **Crook and awning read as a palm.** The trunk is a straight vertical pole with fronds bursting from the top.
   - Bow the trunk: at height 4.0 push it 0.35 out toward +X and +Z. Make the taper visible, 0.45 at the base to 0.2 at the tip.
   - Leaves: 2.2 long, **0.95 wide, rounded tips** like the machine's sprout (now narrow and pointed). Attach them 0.5 below the apex on the back side, 35 degrees apart, drooping no more than 15 degrees.
   - Leaf colour `#63AD68` (reads as deep emerald). Crook `#A47E50` with bark patches only (reads as mid-brown with heavy grain).
2. **Tucked blanket** covers about 25% and ends in a pointed pennant. Raise the top edge to 45% (y 3.1), 0.15 higher on the west side. The spill becomes a soft rounded flap 1.8 wide, 0.5 over the rim, no point. Right now ready looks more covered than tucked.
3. **Woven nest is too dark.** Strand base `#6B4A2C`, highlights `#7D5A38`; keep `#3A2616` to the gaps only. Twig ends read as black thorns: 0.12 thick at the root, blunt 0.07 ends painted `#A47E50`, lengths varied 0.25 to 0.6.
4. **Moon, my spec's error:** from the front its horn touches the trunk. Move the centre to (+2.0, 4.25, -0.5), cord and crook tip to follow. Enlarge to **1.0 tall, 0.75 wide**; at the path mouth it is the landmark's signature and is too small.
5. **Blanket colour and stitch.** Teal `#6FB3AE` (reads cyan, the folded one most). Stitch ticks 0.06 long at 0.15 spacing (now castle crenellations). The sunflower star patch is not visible in any state: put it on the front spill, 0.35 across.
6. **Collar.** Eight clean lobes with a clear notch between, each dropping to 1.55. Remove the cream slabs showing under its lower edge; they read as torn paper. Keep at most two chips, 0.15 wide.
7. **Band.** Moons 0.28 tall, dots 0.12 (now about 0.2 and 0.08).
8. **Feet** are tucked under the middle: move them to radius 1.35.
9. **Mat** reads as a target: coil in `#C9A560` and `#B8974F`, the dark `#8A6A3A` only as a thin line.
10. **Folded blanket** reads as three books: bevel 0.08, one rounded fold edge.
11. **Straw bed:** paint the egg dent (oval 1.3 x 1.1, `#8A6A3A` at 30%).
