# Fable review: player scale (2026-11-26)

Judged from the five Studio captures only. Pixel measures are from the 1649x994 frames; stud figures assume a ~5.5-stud avatar at 1.0.

## Verdict: keep 0.8 as the default

| Scale | Avatar in den frame | 18-stud tree | 7-high outcrop | Bear vs avatar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | ~270 px (27% of frame) | 3.3 avatars | 1.3 | ~0.35 |
| 0.8 | ~215 px (22%) | 4.1 | 1.6 | ~0.44 (interpolated) |
| 0.7 | ~190 px (19%) | 4.7 | 1.8 | ~0.53 |

- At 1.0 the avatar fills the den mouth and the cliff reads as a rock garden.
- At 0.8 the mouth clears the head by roughly one more avatar height and the cliff becomes a cliff. The avatar's outfit and hair still read.
- At 0.7 the island gains little more, but the Bear reaches half the avatar's height. It stops being a baby, and the avatar starts to look like a toddler.
- 0.75 is the floor. 0.85 gives back half the gain. Do not ship below 0.75.

## At 0.8

- **Deck in hand:** fine. It spans ~78% of shoulder width (145 px vs 185 px), chunky and toy-like, which suits the handheld hook. 80% is the ceiling. If the deck does not scale with the avatar, that is another reason not to go to 0.7 (~90%).
- **Bear:** better than before. It reads knee-to-hip high, more present. `den-scale-0.8.jpg` has no Bear in it, so the 0.44 is interpolated; `with-bear-0.8.jpg` has the Bear further back.
- **Cave mouth:** good, about 2.3 avatar heights. Not shown: den furniture. Stool seats should stay at or under 1.5 studs.
- **Camera:** not judgeable; these are fixed-camera shots. Expect the follow camera to need zoom distance x0.85 to 0.9 and a lower focus, or the avatar shrinks on screen twice.

## Pets

Do not scale them. The 0.8 avatar already makes them 1.25x larger by comparison, which is the right direction for the stars of the game.

## Top Lawn toys

Keep x1.25. Avatar scale does not change the toy-to-tree or toy-to-outcrop ratio, which was the complaint. Cap any carried toy at ~2.2 studs (half the 0.8 avatar). No toy capture was supplied, so this is unverified by eye.
