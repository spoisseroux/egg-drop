- `/freshsave` in chat, then rejoin, for a new player's first minutes. `/freshsave restore` brings your save back.
- Switches: `/onboard egg3d|stork|safecard|guide off` (and `on`, `clear`); `/alive balls|crunch|air|fruit|keepout|wobble off`;
  `/netsim 600 150 5` fakes a bad connection on the deck (`/netsim off`).
