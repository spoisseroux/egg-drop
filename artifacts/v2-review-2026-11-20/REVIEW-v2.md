# v2 morning review (2026-11-20)

Everything below is in **Pocket Pals Beta** (private, your own fresh save; Robux shop items do not work there). The live game is still v942 (counters only). Contact sheets: `sheet-1` to `sheet-6` in this folder; the full-size captures are in the folders named on each tile.

## What to try, and what to look for

1. **Stats** (sheet 1). Companions > Stats, or Y in pet focus. Six Pocket Buddies tiles, 2x Bonus tags, total level and Keeper tier. Play a round: the results Stats strip and the level-up line after it. Inspect another pet: Stats tab and Compare.
   - Do the icons read as cute and clear? Is Jump (spring) and Speed (sneaker) right?
2. **Stat Eggs and feeding** (sheet 2). Hatch eggs > Stat Egg (15 Stars, 3 a day, one free after your first round each day). Feed now always feeds the pet that is out. Keep puts it in the pouch (Stats > Snacks, or the tray in pet focus: drag onto the pet).
   - Is the eating moment satisfying? (Sounds were synthesized; listen.) Do the boosts make sense ("2x for your next 120 XP")?
3. **The Bunny** (sheet 3). Companions > Pets: adopt for 400 Stars (confirm on the second press) or free at 7 stamps. Dress it, dye it, feed it. Favourite stat Jump (+10%).
4. **Toy Shell** (sheet 4). Every menu in the toy style, coloured by your deck; Settings > Menu colours: Match my deck / Classic Shell. Scrolling checker (still with Reduced Motion).
   - Fun enough, still easy to read? Any screen still in the old colours?
5. **Hatch** (sheet 5). Lid in, shakes, cracks, burst, the RARE jackpot (watch and listen), result card with odds, pity and Hatch another (H / gamepad Y), iris out. Tap to skip from the second hatch.
   - Is rare "insane" enough? Is the warm stage vibrant enough?
6. **Names** (sheet 6). Companions > Name: pick one or two pieces, Save; Names Egg (8 Stars); pieces unlocked by stat levels (Speedy at Speed 25 and so on). The name shows over your pet, on its card and in stats.
