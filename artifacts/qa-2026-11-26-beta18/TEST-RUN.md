# Pocket Pals QA run: beta (18)

**Where:** Pocket Pals Beta (place 107705084891479). Live is untouched.
**What's new:** your beta (17) notes, built overnight.
**Commands:** type in chat (only you, only on the beta).

---

## 1. The cliff path, the Play Den, the terrace

- [ ] Find the path cut into the cliff to the left of the cave and walk up it to the terrace. The zigzag ramp is gone. **Does it feel like part of the cliff now?**
- [ ] The cave is the real Play Den now: a timber frame and lanterns at the mouth, and inside, the **Garden Dash gate**, the **Pocket Deck table** and the **notice board**.
  - Press **E** at each one. The station should answer, not your pet.
  - Known: thin seams show in the den walls; the crystals are too bright (needs a mesh pass).
- [ ] Your pet follows you into the den and waits on the lawn while you're on the path.
- [ ] **On the terrace:** look over the garden. It's still greybox art.
- Switches: `/den off`, `/tier off` (moves you down if you're on the terrace or the path), `/den clear`, `/tier clear`.

## 2. The egg machine (it now plays out like a capsule machine)

- [ ] At the machine, buy an egg. The screen slides away, the camera moves to the machine, the dial makes a full turn, *clunk*, the egg rolls into the cup, then the screen comes back with the hatch.
  - Click, tap or press Space to skip to the landing; Space should **not** make you jump. The first egg of each kind can't be skipped.
- [ ] Buy from far away (more than 24 studs): the egg drops into the preview instead, with no camera move.
- [ ] **"Hatch another" twice in a row:** the second buy goes through (it did nothing before).
- [ ] **Sounds** (synthesized placeholders): dial clicks, clunk, roll, landing, the screen moving, a shine. **Crunchy enough? Too loud?**
- [ ] After a hatch, the shadow sits under the item (the Bone and Ball cast one shadow).
- [ ] The circle behind the egg on Hatch eggs lines up at any window size.
- Switch: `/onboard capsule off` gives the old quick crank.

## 3. The Sleepy Nest

- [ ] **Peek (E):** the egg stirs and a little gift picture shows. No card.
- [ ] The **"?"** opens the help card. On PC your cursor is free: click Got it. E also presses it, and doesn't open your pet behind it. Backspace closes it.

## 4. The HUD and bubbles

- [ ] In a park the top chip reads **"Play date 0/3 · +4 ★"** (your daily 3 park games for 4 Stars). "Pets playing" is gone; only "Park full" shows when it's full.
- [ ] The egg counter is now in the **top notification bar** ("Egg 4 h", gold **"Hatch egg!"** when ready; tap it for the way to the nest). It hides while a full screen is open.
- [ ] The Bear's bubbles are flat stickers with drawn icons and no shine.

## 5. The world

- [ ] **River stones:** real stones (wet band, moss, small stones around them, a wake downstream). Walk across without jumping. They show while races are open, like before.
- [ ] **Race start and finish:** one matching set of timber posts on marble-and-gold feet, rope, cloth flags and a chalk line.
- [ ] Clouds drift with the wind and one sometimes crosses the sun; leaves float down the stream; birds peck the ground and fly up when you come close; an echo in the den.
- [ ] **The Hollow:** the park by the cliff is now a round clearing tucked against the cliff, with no kerb. From the cliff path, do its worn patches read as a **paw print**?
- Switches: `/alive scene off` (stones, flags, sky, water, birds), `/alive windbed on` to try the constant wind sound (off by default; placeholder sound).

## 6. Race music (placeholders)

- [ ] Start a race: the garden music fades, a song starts with the countdown and GO lands on the beat. Over the line it drops; the results card plays a win or placing sting; the garden music comes back after.
- **Listen for:** a clean loop, and the song not too loud against the countdown.

## 7. Your other beta (17) bugs

- [ ] **The floating Bunny:** dock or carry your pet, walk up the cliff; it lands on its own, with no put-down arrow.
- [ ] **PC cursor:** Shift Lock is switched off in this build.
- [ ] Switch away from the game during a hatch reveal and come back: the reveal carries on (it froze before).

## Not in this build

ASMR hatch vibration, the night garden song, the race results animation (stars flying), art for the terrace and the den (meshes), pets walking the cliff path.
