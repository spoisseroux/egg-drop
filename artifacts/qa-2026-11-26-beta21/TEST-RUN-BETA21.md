**Beta:** Pocket Pals Beta (place 107705084891479), beta (21).
**Live:** v1203. Live now has the retention changes, the eye fix and the reveal fix.

## 1. A brand-new player on the beta (all switches on)

- [ ] **Welcome round:** a calm Deck screen. You see the board, Next, the mystery egg and the coach card with Skip. Quests, Difficulty, Win pays, Controls, Chain and Score are hidden.
- [ ] **After the Bear:** no 4-card tour. You get the first-Stars toast, the Bear's hello bubble, and pink paw prints with a ring leading to him (an edge chip if he's off screen).
- [ ] **Pet him:** "First hello! +3 ★" with hearts, and the prints fade. Then his own prints lead to the machine. *(Not seen in Studio: my petting input doesn't register there.)*
- [ ] **Round two:** a gold "Welcome bonus +12" row and the next-egg line ("Customization Egg: you have enough!"). Rounds 3 and 4 pay +4.
- [ ] **The Bear's eyes:** both stay round in the close-up and at curious moments.

## 2. The live first session on the beta

- [ ] Type `/onboard follow off`, `/onboard needs off` and `/onboard nest off`, then start a fresh save. You should get live's version:
  - the "Press E near Bear to say hi" line with the prints;
  - "Hatch a toy for Bear";
  - the round-two pill;
  - the TOMORROW row on results;
  - one "Tomorrow:" garden line.
- [ ] `/onboard … clear` puts the beta's version back.

## 3. Live v1203 (Studio Play Solo on the live place, or an alt account)

- [ ] The same as section 2, without any commands.
- [ ] Switch away from the window during a hatch and come back: the reveal carries on.
- [ ] A long-time save (yours): no welcome bonus, no say-hi line. The tomorrow strip still shows.
