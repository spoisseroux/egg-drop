# Pocket Pals QA run: beta (22) to (25), the den, cliff path, terrace and Top Lawn

**Beta:** Pocket Pals Beta, beta (22). Everything in the beta (21) list still applies (`TEST-RUN-BETA21.md`).

## 1. Walk up with your pet

- [ ] From the lawn, take the cliff path left of the den. It's a 6-stud stone path cut into the rock, with a rock wall on the inside, steps on the two flights and a broken stone edge on the outside.
- [ ] **Your pet follows you up**, keeping to the middle of the path. It hops the steps and reaches the terrace about a second after you. Coming down, it comes with you. (Studio: 4 of 4 climbs and descents passed.)
- [ ] If your pet falls far behind, it pops in a few studs behind you, never on the steps.
- Switches: `/tierpets off` makes pets wait at the foot again; `/tier off` brings everyone down to the lawn.

## 2. The Top Lawn (the second park, moved up)

- [ ] On the terrace, the second Pet Park is a grass hilltop: a swing and slide on the cliff edge, a see-saw, a sandbox, a bench facing the garden and one tree.
- [ ] **From the lawn below, can you see the swing bar and slide roof on the skyline?**
- [ ] A play date and a fetch work up there.
- [ ] The old spot by the cliff is the **Paw Glade**: no toys, just the log, stumps, bench and a paw print in clover. **Does it read as a paw from the path?**

## 3. The den

- [ ] Inside it's one warm, dark rock cave with no seams, lit by the lanterns. The card table, race gate and notice board work as before.
