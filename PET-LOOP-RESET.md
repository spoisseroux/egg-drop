# Pet loop reset

**Implementation contract for Egg Drop, place 96451847774961.** Private v241 (7145db9) is the observed baseline. These decisions are implemented and verified in the current branch; publication is recorded in PLAY-BUILD.md. They take precedence over earlier proposals for family leveling, duplicate rewards and the four directional egg offers. The broader garden art pass is a later milestone.

## 1. Collect companions through eggs

Keep the guaranteed welcome hatch, seven stable catalog identities, existing recipes, prices, odds and rare guarantee. A purchased companion egg always consumes its normal cost and updates the existing rare counter from its actual roll. A new result sets exactly its ownership bit. An already-owned result says **Already owned**, grants nothing and leaves equip unchanged unless the player explicitly chooses Meet. A duplicate rare still resolves the rare guarantee. Closing or rejoining reopens the exact committed result. The player can acknowledge it and continue.

Stop granting family marks, updating challenges and promoting from rounds or hatches. Remove the family tab, journal route, family picker, overhead rank/level badge, rank burst, crest geometry/framing reservations, goal copy, rank feedback in reveals and recap, and active family progression action. Preserve independent achievements, daily contracts, wallet, paid hues and the optional charm. Freeze legacy marks, challenges and entitlements. Keep previously earned Gloaming, Copperleaf and Lantern appearance options usable from frozen legacy entitlement facts without an active family selection. Give new owners Gloaming after 3 saved wins, and Copperleaf and the Lantern background after 10 saved wins. Each entitlement passes either its historical earned check or the new saved-win check. Use totalWins, updated exactly once for a committed win, with visible new conditions and no new wallet or purchasable benefit. Migrate historical receipts verbatim, including pending historical duplicates. Present their collection status without paying a new reward or rewriting the original transaction.

## 2. Hatch one Hue Egg, assign it later

Hatch eggs has **Companion eggs** and **Hue Egg** destinations. Companion eggs shows the two real recipes and their outcomes. Remove the redundant bottom garden egg illustration/card. The Hue destination shows one generic graphic, a cream shell with a broad band segmented into Rose, Tide, Citrus and Iris. The title is **Hue Egg**, cost **8 Stars**. Show the one purchase action **Hatch Hue Egg · 8 Stars**. Open the shell after the commit, then show four named hue swatches, an owned-pet picker and the explicit **Apply to [pet]** action. Both choices can be changed until applying. Show the selected pet in Current/After at matching size and light. The next purchase is available after spending the held use. Opening the menu, selecting a swatch and previewing never commit a change.

**One held item.** A saved held egg has one opaque purchase identity. It can be reopened after leaving, reconnecting or hatching a companion. It survives cancel, invalid assignment, insufficient eligibility, max grade and apply/save failure. Do not offer a second egg while it is held. Suppress purchasing when no currently owned supported pet can learn a further grade, showing the reason instead. Only pip (Zivvi) and puddle (Ovelle) have authored channels today. Show those actually owned, including an unequipped pet. A companion reveal in progress retains priority and its exact ID. Ordinary held stock does not prevent a companion purchase. The Hue reveal carries no rarity roll, pet inventory bit, pity effect or implied reward for closing it.

**Apply.** Consume that exact egg and advance the selected base hue by one grade on that pet, capped at III. Select the learned body, clear its active mix and unlock that hue as a secondary on first grade. Preserve the selected secondary and charm visibility. The held item has no color before assignment. A max-grade swatch remains selectable as a learned color but cannot consume an egg. Show **Already at grade III** before Apply. Grade here measures paid pigment strength only.

**Colors on the same surface.** The Hue destination exposes a free **Colors** section even with no Stars or held item. The pet journal shortcut opens this section with its pet selected. Provide learned bodies, independent learned accents, **Original body**, **Default accent**, and a single **Reset to original colors** action that saves both neutral channels and clears any mix for that pet. Original uses the exact authored porcelain/rose or stone/sage palette. Reset never spends a held egg or discards grades, unlocks or the charm setting. Distinguish previewed swatches from a committed selection. A committed free selection immediately updates an already-rendered visitor rig.

**Vivid palette.** Start Rose #FF327A, Tide #008DFF, Citrus #FFCC00 and Iris #9350FF. Trial body strengths of 65%, 84% and 100% against the authored neutral. Respect the common palette resolver for world, comparison and reveals. Grade I should read as the named color at roaming distance. Full grade should match the canonical swatch before lighting. Keep dark eyes/mouth, ivory, cheek and teeth roles intact. Treat swatches/strengths as visual tuning parameters and record final values against actual noon/night renders.

**Yes to mixing.** After a pet has learned two different bases, select two learned swatches in **Mix body** and apply for free. Exactly six unordered pairs exist. Author six stable midpoint color recipes to avoid gray complementary mixes. Suggested Rose/Tide #AC4BFA, Rose/Citrus #FF733A, Rose/Iris #E333BE, Tide/Citrus #00BE77, Tide/Iris #5859F1, Citrus/Iris #EE6997. Each is a fixed equal-parts recipe, irrespective of selection order. Use the lesser learned grade for tint strength. Show both names and the resulting swatch. Store two sorted canonical IDs or a canonical pair key, derive grade from existing rows. Secondary picks remain independent single colors. Selecting a single body or Original clears the active pair. Mixing never creates another unlock, costs nothing, accepts no arbitrary RGB or recursive pair, and consumes no egg.

### Persistence and retry

Extend HatchProgress and HueState within the existing leased update. New-record schema must explicitly distinguish the legacy paid ledger from new purchases. Validate v4 **before** upgrading it. Continue the supported v2/v3 upgrades. Run migration in lease acquisition, default new-only fields to empty, and make a second acquire a no-op. Preserve sparse inventory, equipped identity, nectar-as-Stars, daily, pity, mastery archive, pending hatch, exact per-pet ranks/body/secondary, saved charm visibility and old receipt amounts, quotes, sequences and proof. The existing validator requires every historical acquired grade to have a distinct paid slot. For new assignments prove each grade using the union of immutable legacy purchase slots and successful new assignment slots. Maximum 24 slots total (2 pets × 4 directions × 3 grades). Never fabricate a legacy purchase to satisfy validation. Invalid records fail closed without replacing them. Legacy directional requests may replay an existing matching saved receipt. Reject an unseen old purchase rather than implementing a second shop path.

Derive opaque token from the committed purchase, and prepare operation ID/random choices outside a retried update callback. Keep existing lease, session fencing and save-before-confirmation conventions.

## 3. Pet focus and garden motion

**Public actor and private shot use different transforms.** Currently RoomView stages the owner at plan.origin, GardenClient sends this position, GardenState overwrites from/to with it and PetFollow samples Focus without the usual presentation delay. Back restores a stale pet position. Instead, server samples the current public path at focus acceptance, then pauses the pet at that authoritative position for care. Publish focus/pose and generation while retaining current position and facing. Keep visitor interpolation history through Focus and Back. On exit start from the public paused position and resume a continuous path toward the current owner. Nearby observers keep their own cameras. Focus idempotency, generation, reach, character, pet, mode, pulse and timeout checks still apply. Never accept a client-selected stage as proof of reach. A pet outside care range must approach naturally before focus can award contact, or the request declines with visible feedback. Ground/bench/obstacle checks use existing ground and safe-shot logic.

Prefer framing the actual stationary pet when a supported unobstructed shot fits. Where existing shot planning needs a staging point, use that transform only on the owner's presentation under cover. Acquire an opaque, input-blocking local transition, capture roaming camera and controls once, fade to opaque over about 0.10 seconds, change the local rig/camera atomically, then reveal over about 0.14 seconds. On Back, cover first, cancel rub/pulse, sample the current public actor, restore the roaming camera and input, then uncover. A temporary private presentation must never cause two visible rigs. Reduced motion uses an opaque cut for at least one rendered frame, without lens travel. Never restore a stale pre-focus root. Stale tween callbacks, resize, blocked path, Back during entry, respawn, pet swap, menu/board opening, blur and destroy must release owned locks once and respect the newest camera generation. The existing validated petting route is the only reward path.

Top-right is the floating secondary-colored Dot. Keep its familiar glyph with a visible action label **Hide charm** / **Show charm**, state and accessible name. Provide the same affordance and hit area when hidden, with **Saving…** while pending. Back has its own independent 44×44 target. Keep pet face, rub contact area and both controls disjoint at Largest.

**Walk reads as movement.** Drive gait by actual ground distance in both fallback and authoritative samplers. Start around 0.10–0.15 studs peak-to-trough for the current world-scale babies, about one cycle per 1.3–1.6 studs with a 3 Hz ceiling. Zivvi compresses and rebounds; Ovelle makes a softer bob. Keep soles clear of terrain, ease amplitude through braking, suppress gait at rest/focus/reduced motion. Evaluate actual screen travel from the ordinary roam camera. Breathing, blinking and an independent charm continue to express idle life.

**Idle nearby wander.** With a still owner for roughly 3–5 seconds, an unreserved Room actor can investigate a safe nearby point 2–4 studs from the owner's anchor, pause/look for 1–2 seconds and choose another after a short rest. Alternate with existing toy/rest/greeting opportunities. Choose on the existing server decision tick, cap candidate attempts and keep within a small leash around the owner. Reject wet/unsupported, blocked, wrong-height or station-overlap samples, fall back to rest if none pass. Use the existing shared path, speed and heading planner. Walking, focus, board, swap and leave preempt wandering. Owner and visitors see the same curved stroll and stop. No autonomous care, Stars or network-per-frame behavior.

**Night material study for this build.** The current implementation makes cheeks/glints Neon at low daylight while body geometry stays lit. Test an asset-free, pet-local material treatment on real procedural geometry. Start with selective Neon on the main head/body at night, with role-specific dimmed emission colors sampled from the saved resolved palette. Retain SmoothPlastic on adjacent smaller geometry, preserve darker secondary planes (hands, feet, chin/torso), eyes and mouth, and tune selective body versus head emission separately for each silhouette. No change to saved RGB or scene lighting. Select the gentlest night treatment that keeps body hue bright, eyes dark, face and overlapping parts readable. Smoothly taper role color over dusk and gate coarse material transitions with hysteresis. Fallback if a Neon head loses volume: use SmoothPlastic for head plus limited Neon secondary/body pieces, then compare at identical exposure. Check vivid and neutral originals, not only the brightest swatch. Do not add lights per pet. Reuse preview lighting inside ViewportFrame; inspect the world rendering separately. Masked self-emission is available on suitable textured meshes via SurfaceAppearance emissive mask, tint and strength. The Studio capability probe confirms these properties can be read/written. Procedural parts do not already own an authored emissive map. If an applicable existing asset with a verified usable mask is available, compare it on a compatible mesh in Edit; require an actual rendered benefit. No security changes or new uploads are needed for the primitive comparison. [Roblox PBR texture setup](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/modeling/surface-appearance) · [ViewportFrame](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ViewportFrame)

## 4. PocketDeck, aim and menus

Anchor the launch at board (0, SpawnY) and the on-screen nozzle at that same projected point for each tier. The guide rotates about it. Keep the existing normalized [0,1] submission for input compatibility, map 0.5 to straight down, endpoints symmetrically left/right. Begin tuning at ±35 degrees and speed 10 board units/second. In board Y-up coordinates the launch velocity is (speed × sin(angle), −speed × cos(angle)). Verify reach to both objective banks on real 24-drop rounds, adjust angle/speed together if needed, log final constants. Shared BoardConfig owns mapping, analytic launch vector and guide/pivot. Preview tangent or a short predicted flight using the same physics and actual scale. Pointer/drag, D-pad, keyboard and A share the angle; resize cancels drag. LCD image, native spans, sprite fallback and first-session guidance must all draw the same center and direction. Idle/denied drop never moves the pivot.

BoardSimulation derives spawn and velocity server-side. Validate normalized finite numeric input, mode, playable phase, cooldown, budget, contact/fallback, tier, spawn overlap and egg cap. A client cannot submit a position, velocity or custom force. Keep deterministic seed/replay and collision/merge/overflow semantics. Regenerate objective-aware solver traces with center-angle physics and recheck the guaranteed starter board; legacy X-spawn win traces represent the previous rule set. Preserve accepted-drop-only sound/FX.

Represent left/right objective **region thresholds** by thin 1–2 px lines like Keep low, behind play pieces, with existing labels, progress and completion state. Remove all region fill from GUI spans, image renderer and sprite fallback. The physical diagonal bank collision geometry remains. Make Play and Daily visually distinct at rest with theme-aware tab backgrounds. Active tab gets a high-contrast edge/underline and selected text state; keyboard/gamepad focus gets a separate ring. Apply consistent active tab treatment in Hatch eggs. Play lists destinations; remove its target egg/progress card and collapse its layout space. Keep run recap egg progress specific to its earned result. Preserve theme-specific identity and readable state changes in grayscale. Every interactive target is at least 44×44 effective screen pixels, and compact screens scroll the choices while keeping the current primary action reachable. Check 350px safe width, short landscape, largest preferred text, notches and controller navigation after the Mastery tab removal.
